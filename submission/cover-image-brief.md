# Cover Image Brief

## Dimensions

- Recommended: 1280 x 720 pixels.
- Alternative: 1600 x 900 pixels.

## Title Text

NHS Pathway Escalation and Evidence Agent

## Subtitle

A secure, evidence-grounded Google ADK demonstration with MCP, reusable Agent Skills and human review.

## Visual Hierarchy

1. Title at top or centre-left.
2. Subtitle below title.
3. Simple architecture line from synthetic case to agents to human review.
4. Evidence and guardrail markers near the agent workflow.
5. Badges along the bottom.

## Suggested Diagram Elements

- Synthetic case card.
- Deterministic calculation block.
- Google ADK agent workflow.
- MCP connector.
- Evidence document icon.
- Guardrail shield.
- Human review approval checkpoint.

## Badges

- Google ADK
- MCP
- Agent Skills
- Human Review
- Synthetic Data

## Prohibited Content

- NHS logo or branding.
- Real patient imagery.
- Medical advice imagery.
- Hospital identifiers.
- Fake certification badges.
- Claims of deployment, clinical approval or NHS endorsement.

## Accessibility

- Use high contrast.
- Keep text large and readable at thumbnail size.
- Avoid red/green-only status encoding.
- Provide meaningful alternative text.

## Colour-Neutral Guidance

Use a restrained professional palette with neutral background, blue or teal accents and clear status markers. Avoid a single-colour monotone design.
